# Workflow 10 — Competitive Listing Intelligence
Objective
Understand what competitors demonstrate about demand, positioning and customer expectations without blindly copying them.

Analyse selected competitors across:

name

subtitle

category

keyword positioning

icon

screenshot 1

screenshot sequence

feature claims

value propositions

social proof

pricing communication

subscriptions

trial structure

reviews

rating

localization

CPP usage where observable

significant listing changes

Distinguish:

competitor behaviour

from

evidence the behaviour works.

Look for:

market consensus

positioning gaps

overused claims

underserved use cases

recurring visual patterns

repeated keyword families

weak competitor propositions

complaints the market has not solved

potential differentiation

Output
Market Consensus
Meaningful Differences
White-Space Hypotheses
Claims Everyone Uses
Claims We Should Avoid
Creative Patterns Worth Testing
Competitor Weaknesses Worth Exploiting Through Product or Positioning
Ideas Requiring Validation
Never infer competitor conversion or profitability without evidence.
