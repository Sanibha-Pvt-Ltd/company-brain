# Workflow 8 — Review & Voice-Of-Customer Miner
Treat reviews as both:

product intelligence

and

language intelligence.

Analyse first-party and competitor reviews for:

recurring use cases

customer language

jobs to be done

positive outcomes

purchase drivers

objections

failed expectations

bugs

pricing complaints

subscription confusion

cancellation issues

feature requests

trust concerns

competitor weaknesses

emotional language

unexpected customer segments

Do not assume frequently mentioned phrases are necessarily high-volume search keywords.

Use them as candidates for validation.

Output
Recurring Jobs to Be Done
Language Customers Actually Use
Strongest Purchase Drivers
Biggest Objections
Product Problems Affecting ASO
Keyword Hypotheses
Screenshot / Messaging Ideas
Competitor Weaknesses
Product Roadmap Inputs
Distinguish between:

ASO problem

and

product problem masquerading as an ASO problem.
