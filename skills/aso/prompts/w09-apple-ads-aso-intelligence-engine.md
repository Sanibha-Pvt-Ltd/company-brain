# Workflow 9 — Apple Ads → ASO Intelligence Engine
Objective
Use paid-search data as demand and conversion evidence for organic App Store decisions.

Where available analyse search terms using:

impressions

taps

TTR

installs

conversion rate

spend

CPA

trial rate

subscription conversion

revenue

D7 revenue

D30 revenue

LTV

ROAS

Do not prioritise terms purely because their paid-search volume is high.

Classify search terms:

Organic Priority
Strong relevance + strong conversion + strong economics.

Metadata Candidate
Good product fit and promising downstream behaviour.

CPP Candidate
Strong intent but requiring different messaging.

Product Opportunity
Demand exists but current experience does not satisfy it sufficiently.

Paid Only
Economically useful but inappropriate for organic metadata.

Negative / Ignore
Weak relevance or poor economics.

Output
For every important search term:

Term

Paid demand evidence

Install conversion

Trial conversion

Paid conversion

Revenue quality

Current organic rank

Current metadata presence

Recommended ASO action

Confidence

Then provide:

Highest-Value ASA → ASO Transfers
CPP Opportunities
Metadata Opportunities
Product Opportunities
Terms Not Worth Organic Pursuit
