# Stage B — Market

Run once per category after stage A notes exist for most assigned apps. Output: `research/categories/<cat>/market.md`.

Inputs: every `research/apps/*/*.md` in the category, `lens.md`, `overview.md`, web research. List apps covered vs assigned up front; flag gaps.

Write `market.md`:

1. **Size & trend** — US monthly revenue and downloads for the category, 24-month trend, growth rate; sources with ranges `[ESTIMATE:…]`.
2. **Concentration** — top-3 share; health of the long tail.
3. **Landscape table** — app | est. rev/mo | est. dl/mo | rev per download | rating/count | model | price | positioning | last update.
4. **Who wins and why** — distribution vs product vs monetization vs brand; cite app notes.
5. **New-entrant proof** — apps launched in the last 24 months that reached meaningful revenue: how fast, what wedge. No winners = a warning sign; say so.
6. **Pricing benchmarks** — weekly/monthly/annual/lifetime ranges, trial lengths, dominant structure.
7. **Cross-app failure matrix** — rows: complaint clusters from stage-A A6 tables; columns: apps; cells: % of that app's 1–2★ reviews. Highlight clusters hitting ≥2 apps that stayed unfixed for 12 months. Rank by frequency × severity. This feeds MVP differentiators.
8. **Keyword opportunity** — high-intent terms leaders under-serve `[INFERRED]` unless sourced.
9. **Ad landscape** — who spends, which angles are saturated, which untried.
10. **Unit-economics sketch** — plausible CPI vs revenue per download → is paid acquisition viable or must we win on ASO/organic? Label assumptions.
11. **Entry verdict for 2026** — go / no-go / go-with-wedge, the wedge in one sentence, confidence (low/med/high) and why.
