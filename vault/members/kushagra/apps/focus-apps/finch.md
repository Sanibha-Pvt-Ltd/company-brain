## Eight lens dimensions

1. **First win:** A first task/check-in may occur when the starter plan appears (p.30–31), but no completed goal state is evidenced before then. A Day 1 streak screen appears later (p.36–37); it is a capture, not verified outcome or timing. Exact screens/taps and whether the goal is completed before trial are [UNKNOWN].
2. **Ask ledger:** egg/pet/pronoun/trait/name asks (p.1–8); reminder opt-in (p.11); profile and mental-health/support questionnaire (p.13–29); then trial/one-time premium offer (p.32–35); streak goal and widget ask later (p.38–39). Screens are not a confirmed clickstream, but show extensive asks before plan. Benefit received by p.30 is a customized starter-goal list.
3. **Abstractions:** pet + pronouns/traits, areas, goals, activities, reflections, streaks, currencies, seasonal quests, Plus (p.1–65). The starter task is legible; the wider system could overwhelm users if surfaced all at once. [INFERRED]
4. **Feel-good:** Hatching, naming, Finch thanking user, heart animation, compassionate micro-goal plan, and first streak celebration (p.4–10, p.30–37) tie feedback to an actual selection/action. These are more earned than a fake analysis spinner, though the 45M+ claim and reviews are social proof rather than user progress.
5. **Feel-bad:** Deep personal quiz before a first check-in (p.13–29) may feel invasive. “Unstoppable streak” framing (p.38) could create pressure. One-time offers state urgency (“Offer expires when you exit this screen!” p.34; “Expires in 4h 18m” p.57); no evidence of a blocked close on those offer screens. Trial term inconsistencies need reconciliation before purchase.
6. **Paywall:** Captures show Plus pitch after starter goals and again in shop/settings. Offer surfaces differ: p.34 says 14-day trial and later charge on Oct 17; p.57 says 12 months ₹399/year after a 7-day free trial and monthly ₹99 with 3 days free; Apple sheet p.59 says one-week trial then ₹399/year starting Oct 10, 2026. Treat all as captures with ₹ prices; storefront not independently verified and distinct observed offers; do not merge. Screens have Skip/Maybe later or X affordances on some offers.
7. **Repeat cost:** [UNKNOWN] in taps. Home and goals list p.40/p.61/p.64 show direct daily check-ins; goal completion may be one tap, but no interaction recording establishes count. Return cues include reminders, streak, widget, quests, and pet progression (p.11, p.36–45).
8. **Feature map:** Table stakes: small daily actions and progress history (p.30–31, p.61–64). Differentiator: emotionally supportive pet and self-compassion framing, starter plan, optional friend/widget/accountability (p.9–11, p.30–31, p.39, p.45). Bloat risk: exposing mental-health intake, trait taxonomy, currencies, cosmetics, streak commitment, newsletters and insights before the first check-in. [INFERRED]

## Keep / Kill / Different

- **Keep:** Starter goals are concrete and forgiving: get out of bed, drink water, stretch, breathe (p.30–31). Keep Finch’s supportive copy that care can happen “even when you’re not feeling your best” (p.9). Keep optional reminder route (p.11) and visible “Maybe later” / skip on offers.
- **Kill:** Do not require the full mental-health and lifestyle quiz before the first useful action (p.13–29). Don’t use streak pressure as the main definition of progress (p.38), and don’t present conflicting trial durations/prices across in-app/native purchase views (p.34, p.57–59).
- **Different:** Let a user choose one goal and check it off first; ask a short optional question after that to tailor the next suggestion. Show one Plus offer only after a few actual check-ins, with a single consistent trial/renewal contract. [INFERRED]
