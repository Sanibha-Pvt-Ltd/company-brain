## Eight lens dimensions

1. **First win:** The clearest tangible preparation is the saved scan preview (p.12, p.15). No completed send is shown; exact taps and whether first delivery is before payment are [UNKNOWN].
2. **Ask ledger:** sign-up-or-later (p.1), plan selection presented in early pages (p.2–3), email account (p.5), notification opt-in (p.6), document/recipient setup (p.4, p.7), send/receive pricing (p.9–10, p.16). Supplied screenshots do not establish one exact path or whether the early subscription can be dismissed beyond the visible X.
3. **Abstractions:** Separate send vs receive service, own fax number, fax plan tiers, cover page, camera mode, scan/edit and account/iCloud settings (p.2–16). Send/receive distinction maps to separate jobs; separate tier logic and unused settings should not delay a first send. [INFERRED]
4. **Feel-good:** Preview and “Not clear enough?” rescan cue support confidence (p.12, p.15). Review testimony and user counts are social proof rather than achieved user value (p.1–3).
5. **Feel-bad:** “Without it, you may miss key updates” pressures notification opt-in (p.6). High-visibility subscription selection appears before demonstrated task completion (p.2–3), though X and Later controls are visible. [OBSERVED]
6. **Paywall:** Separate send and receive walls (p.2–3, p.9, p.16); India monthly/weekly/yearly prices. No trial or downsell is visible. A receiving number costs ₹999/month in the capture (p.8, p.10); cannot infer plan accessibility in US.
7. **Repeat cost:** [UNKNOWN]. Tabs suggest New Fax, Sent, Inbox, Info (p.7–10) but no sent history or second-send path appears. Capture a successful delivery and return to compose.
8. **Feature map:** Table stakes: scan/import, recipient, cover, send/history. Differentiators: explicit send-only vs receive access and in-editor legibility check (p.2–3, p.12). Bloat/cost: entry account solicitation and push fear copy (p.1, p.6), plus exposing plan choice before proving the send task. [INFERRED]

## Keep / Kill / Different

- **Keep:** Clear scan preview and quality retry cue (p.12); distinguish sending from obtaining a number for receiving (p.2–3, p.8–10).
- **Kill:** “Without it, you may miss key updates” notification pressure (p.6). Do not lead with account or plan selection before a document is ready (p.1–3).
- **Different:** Put a small free/send-first allowance ahead of subscription, with receiving-number purchase shown only after a deliberate “Receive” choice. Ask for account only when needed to preserve a fax. [INFERRED]
