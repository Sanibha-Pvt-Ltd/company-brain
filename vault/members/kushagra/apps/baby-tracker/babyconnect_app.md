## Eight dimensions

1. **First win:** The first potentially useful action is a category entry on p.15 onward, after account/profile choices and the trial offer [OBSERVED]. Exact event completion is not shown.
2. **Ask ledger:** Email/account, child profile, caregiver email, tracker categories, then subscription appear in sequence [OBSERVED pp.2-13]. Caregiver sharing can be declined [OBSERVED p.6].
3. **Abstractions:** Six selectable tracking areas plus separate Family/Professional access tiers [OBSERVED pp.7,11-12].
4. **Feel-good moments:** Tour, personalized profile, and graph access are potential reassurance [INFERRED]; no completed record is shown.
5. **Feel-bad moments:** Tutorial interrupts the dashboard across pp.14-21 [OBSERVED]; cost is repeated orientation [INFERRED].
6. **Paywall:** Trial is shown before first personal log; full-res pp.11-13 show price/renewal and X on offer, followed by Apple confirmation [OBSERVED]. How X behaves is UNKNOWN.
7. **Repeat cost:** Plus button and individual category entry are visible [OBSERVED pp.15,34]; taps to save/repeat are UNKNOWN.
8. **Feature map:** Must match: quick category event capture and history [OBSERVED]. Differentiation: caregiver sharing and charts [OBSERVED]. Bloat risk: professional mode and many unrelated categories for a single caregiver [INFERRED].

## Keep / Kill / Different

- **Keep:** Keep the guest option on registration (p.2) and direct event tiles (p.15).
- **Kill:** Kill: the trial before an initial saved event (pp.8-13) and the seven-screen tutorial (pp.14-21) as onboarding that should be skippable; the captures do not prove whether it is mandatory.
- **Different:** allow guest to save one event, then show a concise, skippable tour and offer account sync after that event.

## Limits

These are screenshot observations, not a live usability test. Tap count/time-to-value, conditional branches, saved-state confirmation unless captured, purchase success, reminder delivery, sync behavior, and free access after closing a paywall are UNKNOWN. Any recommendation above is tagged as inference.
