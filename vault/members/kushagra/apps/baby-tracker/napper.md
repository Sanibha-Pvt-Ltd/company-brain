## Eight dimensions

1. **First win:** The tutorial depicts a wake log, nap prediction, and schedule (pp.31-45) after account/trial screens; no screenshot confirms a saved event or realized sleep outcome.
2. **Ask ledger:** Referral, sleep goals, child birth/name, first-born, snapshot, parent name/age/role, family, account, notification/trial sequence (pp.2-26) [OBSERVED].
3. **Abstractions:** Sleep science, pressure, schedule, sound library, course, statistics, Unlimited [OBSERVED pp.19-23,48-65].
4. **Feel-good moments:** “Crunching your data” and team congratulations [OBSERVED pp.18,17]; generated result quality unknown.
5. **Feel-bad moments:** Several profile questions plus lengthy science carousel precede an account/paywall (pp.2-25) [OBSERVED; friction INFERRED].
6. **Paywall:** Trial before first logging tutorial (pp.25-30); full res confirms ₹0 now/₹3,999 later and reminders; no delay evidenced, dismiss X visible.
7. **Repeat cost:** Central plus/event log shown after onboarding (pp.45-46); exact taps unknown.
8. **Feature map:** Must match: quick sleep interval capture. Differentiator: wake prediction and sleep schedule. Bloat: science pages, courses, sound library, six premium-locked trend tabs [INFERRED].

## Keep / Kill / Different

- **Keep:** Keep explicit trial timeline including day 5 reminder and day 7 charge (p.25); keep sleep log visible in tutorial.
- **Kill:** Kill: upfront referral/demographic survey and paywall before first usable log (pp.2-30). Never imply “Unlimited happy baby!” is guaranteed.
- **Different:** ask birthdate only if age-specific prediction is selected; offer optional account after first log and defer subscription until first trend/prediction.

## Limits

These are screenshot observations, not a live usability test. Tap count/time-to-value, conditional branches, saved-state confirmation unless captured, purchase success, reminder delivery, sync behavior, and free access after closing a paywall are UNKNOWN. Any recommendation above is tagged as inference.
