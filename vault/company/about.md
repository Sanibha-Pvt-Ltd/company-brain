# About Sanibha

Sanibha Pvt. Ltd. is a technical service provider that designs and develops apps for the Apple App Store, primarily for US users. The company is new (founded 2026); the team is four people — see [[company/team]].

## What we are doing now

Choosing what to build. The team has gathered competitor screenshots and manual research across 12 app categories. The research agent turns that into an evidence-backed MVP recommendation per category, then a ranking across categories. See [[company/categories]] and [[research/README]].

## How we work

- This brain is the single shared memory: research, decisions, specs. If it isn't here, it didn't happen.
- Research is evidence-first: every claim tagged, every number sourced and dated, unknowns stated. Details in `skills/app-researcher/SKILL.md`.
- Web data first (no paid market-data subscriptions yet). Competitor weaknesses are mined from 1–2 star App Store reviews.
- iOS only. Small team, so each app's MVP should be buildable in about 6 weeks.
- MVPs are drafts until the team reviews and Bharat signs off; sign-offs go in [[logs/decisions]].
- Each member writes only in their own folder; admins promote drafts to canonical notes. See [[members/README]].
