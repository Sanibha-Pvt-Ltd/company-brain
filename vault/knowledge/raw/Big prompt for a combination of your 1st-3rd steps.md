IMPORTANT RESEARCH PRINCIPLES
Avoid these common mistakes:

1. Don't confuse a large market with an attractive startup market.
2. Don't recommend a product simply because existing companies generate lots of revenue.
3. Don't assume subscription revenue means attractive economics.
4. Don't ignore churn.
5. Don't use lifetime LTV alone.
Pay particular attention to D7 and D30 cash recovery.

6. Don't ignore acquisition scalability.
A product with $500 LTV but no scalable customer acquisition is less interesting.

7. Don't over-index on novelty.
A boring product with proven demand and better distribution may be superior.

8. Don't automatically reject competitive categories.
Competition can prove willingness to pay.

9. Distinguish:
good product
good app
good business
good venture opportunity
good performance-marketing business
They are not the same.

10. Work backwards from $1M/month.
The purpose of the research is not to understand the industry.

The purpose is to decide:

What should we build?
